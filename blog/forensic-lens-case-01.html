CASE 01 — The Value Mismatch

THE FORENSIC LENS — See what others miss. Hi, I'm Lisa — a third-generation criminal investigator and forensic coach. Now I examine not crime scenes, but the invisible patterns that shape clarity, leadership, relationships, and success.

The Scene

At a workshop, a manager said: “They just don't care about money anymore.” In the same room sat a young employee who felt completely unseen.

The Pain

Motivation became harder to grasp. Raises did not land. Leaders kept asking: what is the lever?

The Observation

Two generations. Same workplace. Different value systems. Same words, different meanings.

The Pattern

The issue was not only money. It was meaning, recognition, and autonomy. Incentives were optimised, but identity needs were ignored.

The Resolution

The leader reframed value: clearer purpose, visible contribution, more agency in execution. Engagement increased because people felt relevant again.
