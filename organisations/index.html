Forensic Lens: Making the Invisible Visible

Forensic Lens is a diagnostic framework rooted in systems thinking. It treats organisations as living systems shaped by history, relationships, and context. Rather than prescribing solutions, it illuminates dynamics.

01

Historical Inquiry

Examining founding stories, transitions, and inherited legacies that shape current behaviour.

02

Relational Mapping

Identifying patterns of inclusion, exclusion, loyalty, and belonging across the system.

03

Contextual Analysis

Understanding how external pressures and internal structures interact to create dynamics.

04

Pattern Recognition

Detecting recurring behaviours and systemic entanglements that limit performance.
