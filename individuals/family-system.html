What We Do Together

Family is the only system that we don't have to perform to belong to. It's also the one we cannot simply leave. That makes it powerful and at times energy-sapping. The good news: it is not set in stone. You can change your relationship to your family system — and knowing where you come from helps you choose where you want to go.
